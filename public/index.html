Setup Guide

1
Create a Pushover Account

Sign up at pushover.net - 30-day free trial, then a $5 one-time fee

2
Get Your User Key and App Token

Copy your User Key from the dashboard, then create an application to get an App Token

3
Run the Setup Script

In your terminal inside the project folder: node setup.js

4
Install Pushover App on Your Phone
Google Play App Store
5
Press the Button and Brace Yourself!

The siren will play and repeat until you tap Acknowledge in the Pushover app
